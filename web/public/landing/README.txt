Landing page photo montage
==========================

Put 12 animal photos in this folder, named:

  01.jpg  02.jpg  03.jpg  04.jpg  05.jpg  06.jpg
  07.jpg  08.jpg  09.jpg  10.jpg  11.jpg  12.jpg

They fill the hero background left to right, top to bottom (6 across on a wide screen).
Tips:
- Square or close to square works best; they are cropped to fill each tile.
- About 600-800 px wide is plenty. Keep each file under ~200 KB so the page loads fast.
- Use photos you are allowed to use: your own, or free ones from unsplash.com or pexels.com.
- A missing file just shows a green tile, so you can add them a few at a time.
